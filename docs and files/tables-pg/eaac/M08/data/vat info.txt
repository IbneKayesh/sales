SCENARIO 1 — PURCHASE VAT-INCLUSIVE / SALE VAT-EXCLUSIVE

Purchase:
- Supplier price (VAT inclusive): 100.00
- Net purchase value: 86.96
- Input VAT: 13.04
- Total paid to supplier: 100.00

Purchase Entry:
DR  Inventory/Purchase       86.96
DR  Input VAT                13.04
CR  Supplier/Cash           100.00

Sale:
- Selling price (VAT exclusive): 150.00
- Output VAT @ 15%: 22.50
- Customer pays: 172.50

Sale Entry:
DR  Customer/Cash           172.50
CR  Sales                   150.00
CR  Output VAT               22.50

VAT Settlement:
- Output VAT: 22.50
- Less Input VAT: 13.04
- VAT payable to Government: 9.46

Entry:
DR  Output VAT               22.50
CR  Input VAT                13.04
CR  VAT Payable               9.46

When Government is paid:
DR  VAT Payable                9.46
CR  Bank                       9.46

============================================================
SIMPLE SHOP ACCOUNTING DATABASE STRUCTURE
============================================================

Purpose:
A simple database structure for a shop covering:

1. Item Master
2. Purchase Master
3. Purchase Details
4. Sales Master
5. Sales Details
6. Journal Master
7. Journal Details

The system supports:
- VAT Inclusive
- VAT Exclusive
- VAT Exempt
- Input VAT
- Output VAT
- Purchase accounting
- Sales accounting
- CR/DR journal entries


============================================================
1. ITEM MASTER
============================================================

Table Name: ItemMaster

Purpose:
Stores the basic information and default VAT settings of each
product/item.

Columns:

ItemID
    Primary Key.
    Unique ID for the item.

ItemCode
    Unique product/item code.

ItemName
    Name of the product.

PurchasePrice
    Default purchase price.

PurchaseVATRate
    VAT rate applicable when purchasing this item.
    Example: 15.00

PurchaseVATType
    How the purchase price is treated:
    - INCLUSIVE
    - EXCLUSIVE
    - EXEMPT

SalesPrice
    Default selling price.

SalesVATRate
    VAT rate applicable when selling this item.
    Example: 15.00

SalesVATType
    How the selling price is treated:
    - INCLUSIVE
    - EXCLUSIVE
    - EXEMPT

IsActive
    Indicates whether the item is currently active.


Suggested Structure:

ItemMaster
------------------------------------------------------------
ItemID              PK
ItemCode
ItemName
PurchasePrice
PurchaseVATRate
PurchaseVATType
SalesPrice
SalesVATRate
SalesVATType
IsActive
------------------------------------------------------------


Example:

ItemID:              1
ItemCode:            P001
ItemName:            Product A
PurchasePrice:       100.00
PurchaseVATRate:     15.00
PurchaseVATType:     INCLUSIVE
SalesPrice:          150.00
SalesVATRate:        15.00
SalesVATType:        EXCLUSIVE
IsActive:            1


IMPORTANT:
ItemMaster contains DEFAULT settings.

The actual VAT rate/type used in a transaction should also be
saved in PurchaseDetails or SalesDetails so that old invoices
do not change if the ItemMaster is changed later.


============================================================
2. PURCHASE MASTER
============================================================

Table Name: PurchaseMaster

Purpose:
Stores the header/general information of a purchase invoice.

One PurchaseMaster record can have many PurchaseDetails records.

Columns:

PurchaseID
    Primary Key.
    Unique purchase transaction ID.

PurchaseDate
    Date of purchase.

SupplierID
    Supplier/vendor ID.

InvoiceNo
    Supplier invoice number.

SubTotal
    Total purchase value before VAT.

VATAmount
    Total input VAT on the purchase.

GrandTotal
    Total invoice amount including VAT.

PaidAmount
    Amount paid to supplier.

Status
    Example:
    - DRAFT
    - POSTED
    - PARTIAL
    - PAID
    - CANCELLED


Suggested Structure:

PurchaseMaster
------------------------------------------------------------
PurchaseID          PK
PurchaseDate
SupplierID
InvoiceNo
SubTotal
VATAmount
GrandTotal
PaidAmount
Status
------------------------------------------------------------


Example:

PurchaseID:          1001
PurchaseDate:        2026-08-20
SupplierID:          25
InvoiceNo:           SUP-00125
SubTotal:            86.96
VATAmount:           13.04
GrandTotal:          100.00
PaidAmount:          100.00
Status:              PAID


============================================================
3. PURCHASE DETAILS
============================================================

Table Name: PurchaseDetails

Purpose:
Stores the individual products purchased in a purchase invoice.

One PurchaseMaster can contain many PurchaseDetails.

Columns:

PurchaseDetailID
    Primary Key.

PurchaseID
    Foreign Key to PurchaseMaster.

ItemID
    Foreign Key to ItemMaster.

Quantity
    Quantity purchased.

UnitPrice
    Unit price used in this transaction.

VATRate
    Actual VAT rate used for this transaction.

VATType
    Actual VAT treatment used:
    - INCLUSIVE
    - EXCLUSIVE
    - EXEMPT

VATAmount
    Actual input VAT amount for this line.

LineTotal
    Total amount for this line including VAT where applicable.


Suggested Structure:

PurchaseDetails
------------------------------------------------------------
PurchaseDetailID    PK
PurchaseID          FK
ItemID              FK
Quantity
UnitPrice
VATRate
VATType
VATAmount
LineTotal
------------------------------------------------------------


EXAMPLE 1:
Purchase price = 100 VAT INCLUSIVE

VAT = 100 × 15 / 115
VAT = 13.04

Net purchase = 86.96

PurchaseDetails:

PurchaseDetailID:   1
PurchaseID:         1001
ItemID:             1
Quantity:            1
UnitPrice:           100.00
VATRate:             15.00
VATType:             INCLUSIVE
VATAmount:           13.04
LineTotal:           100.00


EXAMPLE 2:
Purchase price = 100 VAT EXCLUSIVE

VAT = 100 × 15%
VAT = 15.00

Total = 115.00

PurchaseDetails:

Quantity:            1
UnitPrice:           100.00
VATRate:             15.00
VATType:             EXCLUSIVE
VATAmount:           15.00
LineTotal:           115.00


EXAMPLE 3:
VAT EXEMPT

UnitPrice:           100.00
VATRate:              0.00
VATType:             EXEMPT
VATAmount:            0.00
LineTotal:           100.00

============================================================
6. JOURNAL MASTER
============================================================

Table Name: JournalMaster

Purpose:
Stores the header of an accounting journal.

The actual Debit/Credit lines are stored in JournalDetails.

A journal can be generated from:
- Purchase
- Sale
- Supplier payment
- Customer receipt
- VAT settlement
- Expense
- Other accounting transactions


Columns:

JournalID
    Primary Key.

JournalDate
    Date of accounting transaction.

ReferenceType
    Source transaction type.
    Examples:
    - PURCHASE
    - SALE
    - PAYMENT
    - RECEIPT
    - VAT
    - EXPENSE

ReferenceID
    ID of the source transaction.

Description
    Description of the journal.


Suggested Structure:

JournalMaster
------------------------------------------------------------
JournalID           PK
JournalDate
ReferenceType
ReferenceID
Description
------------------------------------------------------------


Example:

JournalID:           5001
JournalDate:         2026-08-20
ReferenceType:       PURCHASE
ReferenceID:         1001
Description:         Purchase from supplier


============================================================
7. JOURNAL DETAILS
============================================================

Table Name: JournalDetails

Purpose:
Stores the individual Debit and Credit entries.

Every journal must satisfy:

TOTAL DEBIT = TOTAL CREDIT


Columns:

JournalDetailID
    Primary Key.

JournalID
    Foreign Key to JournalMaster.

AccountID
    Ledger/account ID.

Debit
    Debit amount.

Credit
    Credit amount.

Description
    Optional line description.


Suggested Structure:

JournalDetails
------------------------------------------------------------
JournalDetailID     PK
JournalID           FK
AccountID           FK
Debit
Credit
Description
------------------------------------------------------------

============================================================
IMPORTANT DESIGN RULES
============================================================

1. ItemMaster stores DEFAULT VAT settings.

2. PurchaseDetails stores the ACTUAL VAT rate/type used on
   the purchase.

3. SalesDetails stores the ACTUAL VAT rate/type used on
   the sale.

4. Never recalculate old invoices using the current ItemMaster
   VAT rate.

5. Input VAT belongs to the purchase side.

6. Output VAT belongs to the sales side.

7. VAT payable is:

   Output VAT - Eligible Input VAT

8. Every JournalMaster must have one or more JournalDetails.

9. Every journal must balance:

   Total Debit = Total Credit

10. VAT-inclusive calculation at 15%:

   VAT = Total × 15 / 115

11. VAT-exclusive calculation at 15%:

   VAT = Net Amount × 15 / 100

12. VAT-exempt:

   VAT = 0
